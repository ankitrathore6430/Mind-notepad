Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Thank you for using Mind Notepad ("we", "our", or "app"). Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, storage, and third-party advertising services.

1. Personal Data Collection

Mind Notepad does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal identifiable information (PII). We do not require account creation, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords to use the application.

2. Local Device Storage

All notes, checklists, tags, categories, and settings created by the user are stored strictly locally on your Android device using SQLite/Room local database storage. No notes are uploaded to any external server or cloud storage.

3. Advertisements & Third-Party SDKs

Mind Notepad displays non-intrusive advertisements to support the development and maintenance of the free application. We utilize Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to serve advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect and process anonymous device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), general non-precise location, and diagnostic information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more about how Google processes information at Google Advertising Privacy Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

Mind Notepad is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our developer repository.
