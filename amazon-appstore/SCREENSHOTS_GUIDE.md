# Amazon Appstore Screenshots Guide

Amazon Appstore strictly requires **at least 3 screenshots** (up to 10) before you can submit your app.

---

## 📐 Exact Requirements
- **Count**: Minimum 3, Maximum 10.
- **Allowed Formats**: PNG or JPEG.
- **Color Format**: **NO Alpha channel (No transparency)**. Make sure screenshots have solid backgrounds.
- **Allowed Dimensions**:
  - **Portrait**: `1080 x 1920 px`, `720 x 1280 px`, or `800 x 1280 px`
  - **Landscape**: `1920 x 1080 px`, `1280 x 720 px`, or `1280 x 800 px`
  *(All screenshots in a submission must have consistent orientation - portrait recommended for this app).*

---

## 📸 Recommended 3 Screenshots to Capture

### Screenshot 1: Home Dashboard
- **Screen**: Main screen of Mind Notepad.
- **What to show**:
  - Pinned notes at the top with colored tags (e.g., "Work Project", "Ideas").
  - Category selector chips (`All`, `Personal`, `Work`, `Ideas`).
  - Search bar and clean modern Material 3 cards.

### Screenshot 2: Rich Text Editor
- **Screen**: Inside a note editor.
- **What to show**:
  - The `Aa` formatting bar opened at the bottom.
  - Formatted text showing Heading, Bold text, Italic text, and an interactive checklist item.

### Screenshot 3: Dark Mode & Categories
- **Screen**: App switched to Dark Theme or showing search results.
- **What to show**:
  - Eye-friendly sleek dark theme.
  - Vibrant note color tags glowing cleanly on the dark surface.

---

## 🛠️ How to Capture from Streaming Emulator or Phone
1. Open the app in the Streaming Emulator on the right or on your Android phone.
2. Navigate to each of the 3 screens mentioned above.
3. Take a screenshot (using phone volume down + power, or browser screenshot tool).
4. Verify image dimensions are `1080 x 1920` or resize using any free photo tool / Canva.
5. Save as `screenshot_1.png`, `screenshot_2.png`, and `screenshot_3.png` inside this folder before submitting!
